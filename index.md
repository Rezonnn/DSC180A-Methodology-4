**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic for me this quarter has been analyzing the veracity and factuality of news articles using different factors and prompting methods. I found it interesting that an article cannot always be judged accurately from just one perspective. Looking at factors such as political framing, economic context, sentiment, and factuality can give a more complete picture of how reliable an article is. I also thought it was interesting to compare different prompting approaches and see how the way a model is asked to reason can affect its analysis. This made me realize how important the methodology is when using AI to evaluate information.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

For my Quarter 2 Project, I would be interested in investigating how consistently different AI prompting methods can identify bias and factuality across news articles from different political perspectives. I would like to use a larger and more diverse collection of articles and compare how methods such as baseline prompting, Chain-of-Thought prompting, and more structured reasoning approaches perform on the same articles. I would also be interested in checking whether the results stay consistent when the topic or news source changes. This could help show which prompting method is the most reliable for evaluating news content across different situations.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would make is to use a larger and more balanced dataset. In our current project, we analyze selected articles from different perspectives, but using more articles from a wider range of sources could make the results more convincing. I would also make the evaluation criteria more standardized so that every prompting method is judged using the exact same factors and scoring process. This would make it easier to compare the methods fairly and determine whether one approach actually performs better instead of the results being influenced by differences in how the articles were evaluated.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in testing techniques that involve multiple stages of reasoning and self-evaluation. For example, instead of having the model give one answer immediately, it could first analyze an article, review its own reasoning, and then revise the final evaluation if necessary. I would also be interested in comparing results across multiple models or running the same prompt several times to measure how consistent the outputs are. Another technique I would like to explore is combining the AI-generated analysis with quantitative measures such as sentiment scores or other article-level features. This could make the final evaluation less dependent on a single type of analysis and give us a more complete way to measure article veracity.
